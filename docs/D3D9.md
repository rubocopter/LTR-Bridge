# Direct3D 9 research

Status: planned comparison.

Compare two rendering paths on the same test application: a native D3D9 adapter and a dgVoodoo2 conversion to D3D11. Record access to color, depth, transforms, temporal data, resource-copy cost, frame time, resize behavior, and compatibility.

The native route may retain more original renderer information. The D3D11 route may reuse more modern transport code. Neither is selected as the default until measurements exist.
